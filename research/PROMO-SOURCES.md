# Promo claim sources

This audit covers the authored claims in [CARDS, USES and STATS](../promo/tools/render3.py), plus selected feature wording that materially affects the same claims. On-screen text is transcribed from the renderer. Typography and line breaks follow the authored strings.

The public pages cited below were re-fetched with curl on 2026-11-22. **VERIFIED** means the fetched page supports the on-screen wording. **NEEDS EDIT** identifies wording or context to correct. **UNVERIFIED** identifies wording the source did not substantiate. App Store labels are developer declarations and have no displayed publication date. Local scan evidence comes from tracked aggregate notes and matching code.

The card headings also need qualification. Use ?[ DATA THAT MAY BE COLLECTED ]? and ?[ DATA THAT MAY BE USED TO TRACK YOU ACROSS OTHER COMPANIES? APPS AND SITES ]? for developer declarations. Replace ?[ WHAT THEY WERE CAUGHT FOR ]? with ?[ ENFORCEMENT, SETTLEMENTS AND FINDINGS ]? so the heading covers both allegations and adjudicated findings.
