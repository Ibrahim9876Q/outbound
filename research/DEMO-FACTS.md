# Twelve facts for the Outbound demo

1. The Canadian privacy investigation found that the Tim Hortons app collected granular location information even when it was closed. [Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/opc-actions-and-decisions/investigations/investigations-into-businesses/2022/pipeda-2022-001/)
2. In 2023, Canada’s privacy regulator found that Home Depot sent e-receipt transaction data to Meta without customers’ meaningful consent. [Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/opc-actions-and-decisions/investigations/investigations-into-businesses/2023/pipeda-2023-001/)
3. Ireland’s privacy regulator fined Instagram €405 million in an inquiry involving children’s accounts. [Irish Data Protection Commission](https://dataprotection.ie/en/news-media/press-releases/data-protection-commission-announces-decision-instagram-inquiry)
4. Ireland’s privacy regulator announced a €530 million fine against TikTok following its inquiry into transfers of European user data to China. [Irish Data Protection Commission](https://www.dataprotection.ie/en/news-media/latest-news/irish-data-protection-commission-fines-tiktok-eu530-million-and-orders-corrective-measures-following)
5. The FTC’s GoodRx case concerned sensitive health information the company disclosed for advertising and resulted in a $1.5 million civil penalty. [Federal Trade Commission](https://search.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/02/ftc-enforcement-action-bar-goodrx-sharing-consumers-sensitive-health-info-advertising)
6. The FTC’s BetterHelp order bans sharing sensitive health data for advertising and required $7.8 million for consumer refunds. [Federal Trade Commission](https://search.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/07/ftc-gives-final-approval-order-banning-betterhelp-sharing-sensitive-health-data-advertising)
7. The FTC sued Kochava in 2022, alleging that its location data could reveal visits to reproductive health clinics and places of worship. [Federal Trade Commission](https://search.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/08/ftc-sues-kochava-selling-data-tracks-people-reproductive-health-clinics-places-worship-other)
8. In 2025 the FTC finalized an order banning Mobilewalla from selling sensitive location data. [Federal Trade Commission](https://search.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-finalizes-order-banning-mobilewalla-selling-sensitive-location-data)
9. The FTC alleged that Avast collected browsing data through extensions and antivirus software and sold it through Jumpshot. [Federal Trade Commission](https://search.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/02/ftc-order-will-ban-avast-selling-browsing-data-advertising-purposes-require-it-pay-165-million-over)
10. A federal appeals opinion describes a settlement over data collected from Chrome users browsing in Incognito mode. [U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2026/04/20/24-5692.pdf)
11. OpenAI reported that a March 2023 bug let some ChatGPT users see titles from another active user’s chat history. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/march-20-chatgpt-outage/)
12. The Dutch privacy regulator announced a €4.75 million fine over how Netflix informed customers about its handling of personal data. [Dutch Data Protection Authority](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl/en/current/netflix-fined-for-not-properly-informing-customers)
